About BackroomFind Property Rentals

BackroomFind Property Rentals is a small property rental business that connects tenants with affordable long-term accommodation in South African townships and urban areas. The platform allows direct communication between landlords and tenants without the use of agencies or middlemen.

Company History

BackroomFind was established to address the challenge of finding affordable rental accommodation. Many tenants rely on social media and word-of-mouth advertising to find rooms. BackroomFind provides a centralised online platform where tenants can easily browse available properties and contact landlords directly.

Mission Statement

To provide affordable, accessible and reliable long-term rental accommodation through a simple online platform that simplifies communication between landlords and tenants.

Vision Statement

To become South Africa's preferred online platform for long-term backroom rentals and affordable accommodation.

Target Audience

  • Students seeking affordable accommodation
  • Working professionals
  • Families looking for rental rooms
  • Individuals seeking affordable housing
  • Landlords with available rental properties

Team Members

  • Property Owner
  • Property Manager
  • Maintenance Coordinator
  • Customer Support Representative

Website Goals

The primary goal of the website is to provide property listings, room search functionality, room-type filtering, WhatsApp integration, direct calling features, room availability indicators and mobile responsiveness.
