Our Services

BackroomFind Property Rentals provides affordable long-term accommodation solutions for tenants and direct communication opportunities for landlords. Our services are designed to simplify the rental process and improve accessibility to quality housing.

Services Offered

Service Description
Long-Term Rentals Affordable rental rooms for students, professionals, families and individuals seeking long-term accommodation.
Property Listings Browse available rental rooms with detailed descriptions, pricing information and room availability status.
Property Maintenance Regular inspections and maintenance to ensure rooms remain safe, clean and comfortable.
Room Viewings Schedule appointments to inspect rental rooms before making a rental decision.
Tenant Support Direct communication with landlords to resolve rental queries quickly and efficiently.
WhatsApp Support Convenient communication through WhatsApp for enquiries, room availability and support.

Benefits of Our Services

  • Affordable long-term accommodation options.
  • Easy access to property information.
  • Direct landlord communication.
  • No agency or middleman fees.
  • Quick and convenient enquiry process.
  • Mobile-friendly rental platform.
